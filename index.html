Why it matters

Modern cities face a growing stream of complaints. CivicPulse helps turn noise into action.

🧠

Complaint understanding

Uses AI-powered natural language processing to interpret complaint context, extract service issues, urgency, location, and citizen sentiment from unstructured text.

⚡

Priority intelligence

Scores incidents based on impact, severity, recurrence, vulnerable community risk, and public safety implications to highlight what deserves immediate attention.

🔁

Duplicate detection

Identifies repeated reports by matching complaint narratives, locations, categories, timestamps, and service patterns to reduce duplication and improve consistency.

🏛️

Department assignment

Routes every complaint to the most relevant municipal department using rule-based logic, service taxonomy, and AI-based classification for faster resolution.

📊

Decision support

Gives officials explanations, risk summaries, operational insights, and recommended next steps to support evidence-based governance decisions.

📈

Operational visibility

How the platform works

From complaint intake to resolution insight

01

Complaint intake

Citizen complaints arrive via web forms, mobile apps, IVR, WhatsApp, emails, or field reports.

02

AI text understanding

The system parses language, tags categories, detects issues, extracts geolocation, and identifies urgency.

03

Duplicate & risk analysis

Similar complaints are clustered and linked to prior cases, preventing duplicate handling and flagging risky patterns.

04

Smart assignment

AI routes cases to the right government department using category mapping, location rules, and workload capacity.

05

Official decision support

Dashboards show severity, recommended action, prior case history, department context, and field guidance.
